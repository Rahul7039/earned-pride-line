One line of earned pride (134 characters):

> The boy who stammered through every assembly stood up, read his poem to the last line, and the hall forgot to clap for a whole second.

A small person doing something hard: a boy who has stammered through every
school assembly finally reads his poem to the last line — and the pride lands
in the hall's stunned second of silence before the applause. The effort is
inside the line (every assembly), the scene is concrete (a boy, a poem, a
hall), and nothing is asserted, congratulated or explained. The line was
written fresh for this competition; ten candidate lines were drafted and
judged against the brief, and this was the strongest.

`main.py` is pure standard-library Python, fully deterministic. It writes the
single line above to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, UTF-8, trailing newline).
Running it twice produces byte-identical output.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
